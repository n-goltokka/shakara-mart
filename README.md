Title: Develpment of an Advanced Database Systems Portfolio
DB Case Study: Shakara-mart Portfolio 

Shakara Mart is an e-commerce company that sells consumer retail goods.

The company operates an online shopping platform serving customers across Nigeria, with operations in Lagos, Abuja and Benin City.

Customers can browse products, register accounts, place orders, make payments and track deliveries. 
Administrators manage product listings, inventory, customer orders and sales reports.

Business problem
Shakara Mart needs a centralised database system to manage its operations.
Without a properly designed database, the company could experience duplicated customer information, inaccurate stock records, inconsistent payment information, difficulty tracking deliveries and unreliable sales reporting.

The proposed database will provide structured storage, enforce relationships between records and support reliable transaction processing.
